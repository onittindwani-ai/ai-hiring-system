# Glossary

- **Scorecard**: the list of job related criteria and weights written before anyone is scored.
- **Must have / nice to have**: a must has a minimum level; a nice only adds points.
- **Level 0 to 4**: how strongly the text shows a criterion (none, mentioned, with help, on their own, taught or improved).
- **Gate**: a yes or no fact that must be true to proceed (for example the legal right to work). Unknown means a person checks.
- **Tier**: strong, maybe, review or below. Below means not shortlisted now, never an automatic rejection.
- **Blind (redacted) review**: scoring text with names, contact details, links and gendered words removed.
- **Prospect**: someone who could fit but has not applied. **Applicant**: someone who applied.
- **Silver medalist**: a strong finalist you did not hire, kept warm.
- **X-ray search**: a web search that uses site and keyword operators to find public pages.
- **Boolean string**: a search with AND, OR, NOT and quotes.
- **Funnel conversion**: the share of people who move from one stage to the next.
- **Fully loaded cost**: pay plus taxes, benefits, equipment and tools.
- **Prompt injection**: text in a document that tries to command an AI.
- **Jev**: TypeSafe AI's fast decision model (optional here).
- **Employer of record (EOR)**: a company that legally employs someone on your behalf in another country.
