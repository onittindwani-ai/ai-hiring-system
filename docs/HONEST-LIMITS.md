# Honest limits

- This system helps you hire. It does not replace your judgement, a lawyer, an accountant or an employment contract template reviewed where you live.
- Claude does not post jobs, send messages, or log in to job boards for you. You press Post and Send. That keeps your accounts safe.
- It cannot see anyone's private profile and it does not scrape. Prospects found through public search are only the people who published something public. For LinkedIn and Indeed you run the search and bring results back.
- Scores are a structured first pass based on text. They can be wrong. A resume is not the person. Every no and every offer is a human decision.
- Blind scoring removes names, emails, phones and links and swaps gendered pronouns. It cannot remove everything that hints at who someone is.
- Planning numbers (conversion rates, cost shares) are starting assumptions, not market facts. The pipeline replaces them with your real numbers.
- Laws about AI in hiring change often and differ by country, state and city. The law doc is an overview with dates and sources, not advice.
- Jev is optional and independent of this kit. We did not test the live Jev API on candidate data for your market. Test it on a few of your own cases first.
- The sample data is made up: 60 fake candidates for 3 roles. Real applicants will surprise you.
- Prices, platform rules and product features change. Everything with a date was checked on that date.
