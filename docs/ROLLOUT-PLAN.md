# Rollout plan: your first 2 weeks

| Day | Do | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Open this folder in Claude Code, run `/hire-start`, answer the browser question, give name, website, a page | Setup done, company understood |
| 1 | `/hire-plan` for your first role | Plan, budget, funnel numbers |
| 2 | Approve the scorecard, post the job (you paste it) and publish the form | The role is live |
| 2 to 3 | `/hire-source`: run the board searches Claude gives you, paste results | A prospects list |
| 3 to 5 | `/hire-reach`: approve the first batch of messages, press send | Replies start |
| 5 | `/hire-screen` on the first applicants (calibrate on 5 first) | A shortlist |
| 6 to 10 | `/hire-interview`: structured interviews, a work sample | Evidence per person |
| 7 and 14 | `/hire-weekly` | The leak and the one change |
| 10 to 14 | References, offer, close | A hire, or a clear reason why not |

After the first batch the funnel numbers come from your own data, not from starting assumptions.
