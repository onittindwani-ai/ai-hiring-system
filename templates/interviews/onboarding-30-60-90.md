## Rules for fair onboarding

- Give clear feedback every week, in private, about the work and not the person.
- Judge the same way for everyone. Use the outcomes you agreed at the start, not a new test.
- If a trial or probation period applies, say so in writing, say what is expected and check in before the end. Local rules differ. Not legal advice.
- If it is not working, say so early, offer help and write down what changes. Surprises at the end of a trial are unfair.
- Ask the new person what you could do better in your hiring and onboarding. Use the answers.
