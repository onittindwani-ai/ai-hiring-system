## Opening

"Thank you for your time. {{candidate_name_if_allowed}} listed you as a reference for a {{role_title}} role. I will ask about the work you saw them do. What you say stays between the people making the hiring decision. You can skip any question."

## Questions (same for every referee for this role)

1. How do you know the candidate, and for how long did you work together?
2. What was their job, and what were they responsible for?
3. Tell me about one thing they did that you were glad about. What was the result?
4. {{criterion_1_question}} (confirm the main must criterion: "In your view, how did they handle ...?")
5. {{criterion_2_question}}
6. What would help them do well in a role like this? What kind of support or manager works best?
7. Is there something about their work you would want a new manager to know?
8. Would you work with them again? Why?
9. Is there anything else I should ask?

## Rules for asking

- Ask for examples. "Great person" is not evidence. "They rebuilt our schedule and cut late jobs" is.
- Do not ask about health, age, family, pregnancy, religion, nationality or anything not tied to the work.
- Do not ask why they left in a way that fishes for private reasons. Ask what the work was like.
- Do not promise the candidate will get the job.
- Be careful with a very short or cold reference. It may be company policy, not a signal. Check with a second referee.
