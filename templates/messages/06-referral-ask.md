# Referral ask

- **Use when:** You ask a colleague, customer, friend or professional contact if they know a good person for the role.
- **Who sends:** The owner, by hand.
- **Channel:** Email, text or LinkedIn message
- **Length limit:** Under 90 words.

## Message

Hi {{referrer_name}},

I'm hiring a {{role_title}} at {{company_name}}. The person would {{first_outcome}}, and the pay range is {{pay_range}}.

Do you know anyone who might be a good fit? If so, you could pass on this short description: {{role_summary_link}}. No pressure at all, and please only share it with people who would want to hear about it.

Thank you,
{{recruiter_name}}

Reply STOP and I won't message you again.

## Rules

- Ask people who actually know good work in this field. Do not ask for private details about anyone.
- Tell the referrer that the person they name decides for themselves whether to talk to you. Never contact the person on their behalf without it being their choice.
- Thank the referrer, even if nothing comes of it.
- Keep the STOP line when you do not know the person well. You may drop it for a close colleague.
- If you offer a referral bonus, write the amount and the conditions down first and tell the referrer the real terms. Do not invent a bonus.

## Variables used

`{{referrer_name}}`, `{{role_title}}`, `{{company_name}}`, `{{first_outcome}}`, `{{pay_range}}`, `{{role_summary_link}}`, `{{recruiter_name}}`
