# Interview reminder

- **Use when:** One day before the interview.
- **Who sends:** The hiring manager or owner, or a calendar reminder you approved once.
- **Channel:** Email or text
- **Length limit:** Under 50 words (text message limit: 160 characters).

## Message

Hi {{candidate_first_name}}, a quick reminder about your interview for {{role_title}} tomorrow at {{interview_time}} ({{interview_format}}). If anything changed for you, reply here and we will sort it out. {{recruiter_name}}, {{company_name}}

## Rules

- You (the owner) approve the exact text before it is sent. Claude only drafts it.
- Never invent a personal detail. Use `{{specific_detail}}` only for a real fact the person gave you in their application or in the conversation.
- Never promise a job unless this is the offer message and the offer is approved and real.
- No dashes, no hype, no pressure. Plain, warm and short.
- Send only if the person agreed to receive reminders.
- Include a way to reschedule. Never make someone feel bad for asking.

## Variables used

`{{candidate_first_name}}`, `{{role_title}}`, `{{interview_time}}`, `{{interview_format}}`, `{{recruiter_name}}`, `{{company_name}}`
