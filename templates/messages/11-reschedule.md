# Reschedule

- **Use when:** Either side needs to change an interview or call time.
- **Who sends:** The hiring manager or owner.
- **Channel:** Email or text
- **Length limit:** Under 70 words.

## Message

Subject: New time for your {{role_title}} interview

Hi {{candidate_first_name}},

We need to move our {{event_name}} that was set for {{old_time}}. I'm sorry for the change. Could you pick another time here: {{booking_link}}? If none of those times work, reply with a few that do and we will fit around you.

Thank you for your patience,
{{recruiter_name}}

## Rules

- You (the owner) approve the exact text before it is sent. Claude only drafts it.
- Never invent a personal detail. Use `{{specific_detail}}` only for a real fact the person gave you in their application or in the conversation.
- Never promise a job unless this is the offer message and the offer is approved and real.
- No dashes, no hype, no pressure. Plain, warm and short.
- If you are the one moving it, say sorry once and offer at least three new options. If the candidate asked, confirm the new time in writing.
- Do not treat a reschedule request as a negative signal. It is not on the scorecard.

## Variables used

`{{role_title}}`, `{{candidate_first_name}}`, `{{event_name}}`, `{{old_time}}`, `{{booking_link}}`, `{{recruiter_name}}`
