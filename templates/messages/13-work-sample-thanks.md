# Work sample thanks

- **Use when:** You received the work sample.
- **Who sends:** The hiring manager or owner.
- **Channel:** Email
- **Length limit:** Under 70 words.

## Message

Subject: Thank you for the {{role_title}} task

Hi {{candidate_first_name}},

Thank you for the time you put into the task. We have it, and we will read it carefully. You will hear from us by {{reply_by_date}}.

If you would like to add a note about how you worked, you can reply any time before then.

{{recruiter_name}}

## Rules

- You (the owner) approve the exact text before it is sent. Claude only drafts it.
- Never invent a personal detail. Use `{{specific_detail}}` only for a real fact the person gave you in their application or in the conversation.
- Never promise a job unless this is the offer message and the offer is approved and real.
- No dashes, no hype, no pressure. Plain, warm and short.
- Do not comment on quality here. Wait for the scored review.
- Keep your promise on the date.

## Variables used

`{{role_title}}`, `{{candidate_first_name}}`, `{{reply_by_date}}`, `{{recruiter_name}}`
