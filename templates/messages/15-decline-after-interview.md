# Decline after interview

- **Use when:** The person interviewed and you chose another candidate. A person made the decision.
- **Who sends:** The hiring manager or owner, ideally by phone first for finalists.
- **Channel:** Email, or phone then email
- **Length limit:** Under 130 words.

## Message

Subject: Update on the {{role_title}} role

Hi {{candidate_first_name}},

Thank you for the time you spent with us. It was a real pleasure to talk about {{specific_detail}}.

After careful thought, we have decided to offer the role to another candidate whose experience matched our current needs more closely. It was a close decision, and it does not take away from what you showed us.

If you would like short, job related feedback, reply and I will send what I can. If another role opens that suits you, I would be glad to contact you, only if you tell me you want that.

Thank you again, and I wish you the best,
{{recruiter_name}}

## Rules

- You (the owner) approve the exact text before it is sent. Claude only drafts it.
- Never invent a personal detail. Use `{{specific_detail}}` only for a real fact the person gave you in their application or in the conversation.
- Never promise a job unless this is the offer message and the offer is approved and real.
- No dashes, no hype, no pressure. Plain, warm and short.
- Send within the time you promised, ideally a few working days after the decision.
- Offer short feedback only if it is true, job related and agreed by the interviewers.
- Use `{{specific_detail}}` only for something the person really said or did in the interview.

## Variables used

`{{role_title}}`, `{{candidate_first_name}}`, `{{specific_detail}}`, `{{recruiter_name}}`
