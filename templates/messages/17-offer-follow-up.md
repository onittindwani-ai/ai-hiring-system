# Offer follow up

- **Use when:** An offer was sent and the candidate has not answered, or has questions.
- **Who sends:** The owner.
- **Channel:** Phone or email
- **Length limit:** Under 90 words.

## Message

Subject: Questions about the {{role_title}} offer?

Hi {{candidate_first_name}},

I wanted to check in about the offer for {{role_title}}. If you have questions about the pay, the work, the team or anything else, I am happy to talk, and you can book a time here: {{booking_link}}.

Please take the time you need. If our answer date of {{reply_by_date}} is a problem, tell me and we can talk about it.

{{recruiter_name}}

## Rules

- You (the owner) approve the exact text before it is sent. Claude only drafts it.
- Never invent a personal detail. Use `{{specific_detail}}` only for a real fact the person gave you in their application or in the conversation.
- Never promise a job unless this is the offer message and the offer is approved and real.
- No dashes, no hype, no pressure. Plain, warm and short.
- Ask what the person needs. Do not push or imply that other candidates are waiting unless that is true and relevant.
- If they decline, thank them, ask (only if they want to share) what would have made a difference, and move to your backup list in the pipeline.
- Any change to pay or terms needs the owner's yes and a fair view of what the other people in the same role earn.

## Variables used

`{{role_title}}`, `{{candidate_first_name}}`, `{{booking_link}}`, `{{reply_by_date}}`, `{{recruiter_name}}`
