# Welcome after acceptance

- **Use when:** The candidate accepted the offer.
- **Who sends:** The owner or hiring manager.
- **Channel:** Email
- **Length limit:** Under 150 words.

## Message

Subject: Welcome to {{company_name}}

Hi {{candidate_first_name}},

We are delighted that you accepted the {{role_title}} role. Thank you for trusting us with it.

Before your first day on {{start_date}}:

- You will receive: {{paperwork_list}}
- Your first day starts at {{first_day_time}} with {{manager_name}}
- Please bring or prepare: {{what_to_bring}}

Your first 30, 60 and 90 days have a simple plan, and we will go through it together. If something worries you before you start, tell me. I would rather hear it now.

Welcome aboard,
{{recruiter_name}}

## Rules

- You (the owner) approve the exact text before it is sent. Claude only drafts it.
- Never invent a personal detail. Use `{{specific_detail}}` only for a real fact the person gave you in their application or in the conversation.
- Never promise a job unless this is the offer message and the offer is approved and real.
- No dashes, no hype, no pressure. Plain, warm and short.
- Only list paperwork and checks that are real, lawful and told in advance. Check local rules for right to work and background checks. Not legal advice.
- Tell the people who will not be hired, kindly, using message 14 or 15, once the accepted candidate has signed.
- Update the pipeline: `accepted`, then `hired` once they start.

## Variables used

`{{candidate_first_name}}`, `{{company_name}}`, `{{role_title}}`, `{{start_date}}`, `{{paperwork_list}}`, `{{first_day_time}}`, `{{manager_name}}`, `{{what_to_bring}}`, `{{recruiter_name}}`
