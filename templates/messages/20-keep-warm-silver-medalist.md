# Keep warm: a strong runner up

- **Use when:** A candidate was very good but not chosen, and they said they are open to being contacted for future roles.
- **Who sends:** The owner or hiring manager.
- **Channel:** Email
- **Length limit:** Under 90 words. Send at most once every few months.

## Message

Subject: A quick hello from {{company_name}}

Hi {{candidate_first_name}},

I still think about our conversation about {{specific_detail}}. We do not have a role open that fits you right now, but I wanted to say hello and ask how things are going.

If you would like me to tell you when a role like {{role_title}} opens, reply "yes". If you would rather not hear from us, reply STOP and I won't message you again.

Take care,
{{recruiter_name}}

## Rules

- You (the owner) approve the exact text before it is sent. Claude only drafts it.
- Never invent a personal detail. Use `{{specific_detail}}` only for a real fact the person gave you in their application or in the conversation.
- Never promise a job unless this is the offer message and the offer is approved and real.
- No dashes, no hype, no pressure. Plain, warm and short.
- Only contact people who said yes to being kept on file, and only for as long as you told them you would keep their data. Delete the details when that time is over.
- Do not use this as a mass mailing. Write to each person yourself.
- Every message includes the way to opt out.

## Variables used

`{{company_name}}`, `{{candidate_first_name}}`, `{{specific_detail}}`, `{{role_title}}`, `{{recruiter_name}}`
